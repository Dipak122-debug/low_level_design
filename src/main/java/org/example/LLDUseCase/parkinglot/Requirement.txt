Requirements for the Parking Lot System:

- The system should allow users to park and unpark their vehicles in a parking lot.
- The system should allow different types of vehicle and Fees strategy for each type of vehicle.
- The system should be able to handle multiple parking lots and manage their availability.
- The system should support different types of parking spaces (e.g., compact, regular, large) and assign vehicles to appropriate spaces based on their size.
- The system should provide a way to check the availability of parking spaces in real-time.
